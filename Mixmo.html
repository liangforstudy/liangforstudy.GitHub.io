The Curious Gait of a Three-Legged Sergal

Drag to turn him about, pinch to look closer, slide two fingers to move the view.

Game. WASD to run, hold Shift to walk; the arrow keys walk (while paused, ← → step frames); on the robot, Ctrl = his in-between form. On a phone, drag in the left half of the view: a joystick appears under your thumb; push a little to walk, all the way to run. Up = away from the camera. To look around, drag the right half on a phone, or anywhere with a mouse. Psst: try spinning the joystick round.

Six-leg gait:

Applies to the mode picked under "Editing".

180° when on 4, 5 or 6 legs:

Swap ends: he stops and the other end leads; a raised tail moves to the new back end. The variant keeps raised limbs where they are, so it looks like the opposite 5-leg run. Turn round: the body swings round; a shorter body turns faster.

Leaving six legs, stand on:

Back end tips him back onto the end behind him. Front end rises forward over his front legs, so he ends up the other way up, looking exactly the same but with the paw colours swapped. End A / End B pick a paw colour, whichever end it is on.

Power saving:

Draws at most 30 frames a second and stops redrawing while nothing moves. In 3D, shadows become soft spots. Works with 2D and 3D; 2D uses the least power.
